FONT NOTICE (read before a public launch)

This folder holds four web-font files used by the Walking Rotarian site:

  MTNBrighterSans-Bold   headings
  SkyText-Regular        body text
  SkyText-Bold           navigation, buttons, emphasis
  SkyText-Light          large introductory text only (24px and above)

Where they came from
  The files came from the Drive font package, which credits "Web Fonts"
  (https://www.onlinewebfonts.com) and states the licence "CC BY 4.0".
  The site footer carries that credit line. Do not remove it unless the
  fonts are replaced.

What the files themselves say (embedded copyright text)
  MTN Brighter Sans Bold : (c) 2017 Newlyn Works Limited. Licensing: www.newlyn.com/licensing
  Sky Text Regular/Bold  : (c) 2009 BSkyB Ltd.
  Sky Text Light         : (c) 2017 Dalton Maag Ltd. The file states that modification
                           needs prior written permission (www.daltonmaag.com/eula).

What that means
  These are brand typefaces. A "CC BY 4.0" label on a download site does not, by
  itself, give you the owners' permission. The files are fine for building,
  reviewing and a private preview. Before a public launch, confirm you may use
  them, or replace them with fonts you are licensed for.

How to swap them
  1. Put the new .woff2 files in this folder.
  2. Change the @font-face lines at the top of src/assets/main.css.
  3. Run: node build.mjs
  4. Run: python3 -I tools/check_glyphs.py   (checks every character on the site exists in the new fonts)

The font files are not modified by this project.
