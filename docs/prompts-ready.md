## Review gate

- Face outline, eyes, lids and brows, nose, mouth and teeth, beard, complexion, ears where visible, headwrap and neck, compared with the portrait at similar size and angle.
- Reviewed at full size and at the intended phone and desktop size.
- Reject: a polished generic lookalike, a younger version, a widened face, an overgrown beard, a changed smile, or body inflation.
- Site checks: no stray text or numbers, correct hands and boots, no Rotary or club marks, no children, nothing that reads as a real named place.
- Record any limitation (soft or hidden face) instead of inventing detail.
