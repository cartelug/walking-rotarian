# Reference request: places, vehicles and objects

For whoever takes the photographs. The photographs are used as **references** when the website pictures are generated, so the places, vehicles and objects look Ugandan and real. They are **not published**, and **none of them needs Mbanda**.

The folder names match the pictures in `docs/ONE-PROMPT.md` (P01 to P30). When the zip comes back, the files go into a Google Drive folder called `WR website references`, keeping the sub-folders, so that ChatGPT can find them the same way it finds his portrait.

## How to take them

1. Photograph real, ordinary places in Uganda. Not tourist views, not the internet, not AI pictures.
2. No pictures of Mbanda. None of the folders below needs him.
3. Use a phone, the rear camera. No filters, no portrait mode, no beauty mode. Hold it level.
4. Overcast or open-shade light looks best. Take a few at a different time of day if you can.
5. Take photos at different distances and angles: one wide (shows the whole place), one or two medium, one close detail. Do not take five identical shots.
6. Adults only, and only from behind, from the side or far away. If a vendor or rider is close, ask first, and keep their face out of focus. No child's face at all.
7. Keep number plates, phone numbers, shop names and banners out of the picture where you can. If you cannot, also take the same view from another angle.
8. Send the ORIGINAL files (at least 2000 pixels on the long side). Not screenshots. In WhatsApp send them as "Document", not as "Photo", so they are not shrunk.

## What happens next

1. The zip is unzipped into the Drive folder `WR website references`.
2. `docs/ONE-PROMPT.md` gets a new step telling ChatGPT to look in each picture's folder and match the place: soil colour, plants, light, vehicles and objects. It must not copy any person, plate or writing from a reference.
3. The pictures stay labelled "AI-generated illustration", whatever references were used.

## Notes

- A is "must have" because the picture needs it. B is a nice extra. The list is not in any order of difficulty.
- Photos of real places and things may show shop names or plates by accident. Those are never published, but keep them out of the frame when you can.
- Which folders go with which pictures is in the "Used for" column. Several folders feed more than one picture.
